Privacy

What we keep

  • Your account: the email address you sign in with. It is held by our sign-in provider, Clerk.
  • Your lookups: the address you entered, any details you added such as an amount, a date, a transaction ID or the app or website you were told to use, and when you made the lookup.
  • To apply limits and stop misuse: your IP address and browser session, stored only as one-way scrambled codes (salted hashes), never in readable form.

Who can see your lookups

On the site, only you can see your lookups and what you entered with them. The site's operator can access stored lookups in order to run the service.

A report describes an address, not a person. It never shows who looked the address up or what they entered. A report can show a rough band of how many other accounts have looked up the same address, such as "1 to 4". It never shows an exact number, and it never counts your own account.

Companies that process data for the site

  • Clerk runs sign-in and holds your account's email address.
  • Cloudflare delivers the site and runs the check on the lookup form that confirms a person is using it. Every request to the site passes through Cloudflare, including your IP address.
  • Railway hosts the site and its database. Every request to the site also passes through Railway.

What we ask other services

  • To build a report, we ask public blockchain data services about the address you entered.
  • If you enter a website, we ask the registry for that domain (through its public RDAP service) and the public certificate search crt.sh when the domain was registered and when it first had a certificate. We never visit the website itself.
  • These services receive the address or domain name, not who you are. We keep their answers. The answers record what was asked about, not who asked, and they stay when you delete a lookup.

Cookies

  • One session cookie of our own, which keeps the lookup form secure.
  • Clerk's cookies, which keep you signed in.
  • Cloudflare's check on the lookup form, which may set its own.

How long we keep things

  • Lookups are kept for 12 months. After that they are erased automatically, the same way as when you delete one yourself.
  • Your account stays until you ask us to delete it.

Deleting

You can delete any lookup from My lookups. Deleting erases what you entered and the link to your account, and keeps only a record that a lookup of that address happened. To delete your account, write to the address below.

Contact
