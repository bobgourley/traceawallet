Privacy

What we keep

  • Your account: the email address you sign in with, held by our sign-in provider, Clerk.
  • Your lookups: the address you entered and any details you added, such as an amount, a date or a transaction ID. Only you can see these.
  • For limits and abuse prevention: your IP address and browser session, stored only as one-way scrambled codes (salted hashes), never in readable form.

What other people can see

A report describes an address, not a person. It never shows who looked the address up or what they entered. A report can show a rough count of how many accounts have looked up the same address, in bands such as "2 to 5".

Cookies and services

  • One session cookie of our own, to keep the form secure and to attach a lookup to your account when you sign in.
  • Clerk's cookies, which keep you signed in.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that the form is used by a person.

We use no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Deleting

You can delete any lookup from My lookups. Deleting erases what you entered and the link to your account, and keeps only a record that a lookup of that address happened.
