About TraceAWallet

TraceAWallet is a free tool for people who sent cryptocurrency to someone they now think deceived them, and for the people helping them. You enter the address you sent money to. We read public blockchain records and show what they say about it: what it holds, where money went next, where it came from, and whether a stablecoin issuer has frozen it.

What it does not do

  • It cannot recover money, and no one from TraceAWallet will ever offer to.
  • It does not say whether an address belongs to a scam, or to anyone in particular. It reports what records show and what named sources claim, worded as their claims.
  • It follows money one step from the address, not further.
  • It does not yet cover BNB Smart Chain, Polygon or Base.

Where the information comes from

  • Ethereum, Arbitrum One and OP Mainnet: Blockscout.
  • Tron: TronGrid.
  • Bitcoin: mempool.space.
  • Freeze status: read directly from the Tether (USDT) and Circle (USDC) token contracts.
  • Sanctions: the US Treasury's OFAC SDN list.
  • Exchange wallets: lists that OKX, Bitfinex, Bybit, Crypto.com, HTX and KuCoin publish about their own wallets.
  • Address flags: GoPlus Security. Powered by GoPlus

Every report names the sources it used and the date each was read. A claim by a third party, such as an exchange or GoPlus, is shown as that party's claim.

Limits

Public records can be incomplete, and providers can be slow to index new activity. Every report says what was checked, what was not read, and whether the history it read adds up. A report is a snapshot at a stated block and time; it does not update by itself.
